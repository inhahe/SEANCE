Piano Roll

Home

The piano roll shows the notes in a MIDI Timeline clip. Click in empty space to add a note; drag to move; drag the edges to resize; right-click for per-note options. The left column shows piano keys — click one to audition the synth at that pitch.

The toolbar above the grid has a Lane selector showing extra data per note:

  • Velocity — one vertical bar per note spanning its duration. Click+drag a bar to change the velocity of that note.
  • Automation — pick a synth param; see and edit its automation curve as control points with Catmull-Rom smoothing.
