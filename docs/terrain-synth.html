Terrain Synth

Home

Terrain Synth is an N-dimensional synthesis engine: a stored "terrain" (from 1D waveform to 4D hypercube of sample values) plus a traversal that maps time → coordinate and reads the terrain value as audio. Terrains can be built from waveforms, images, audio files, or math expressions; traversals can sweep linearly, orbit, trace Lissajous patterns, or follow user-defined paths.
