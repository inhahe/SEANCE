Wavetables & Layered Waveforms

Home

The Layered Waveform editor lets you construct single-cycle waveforms as a sum of layers (sine, saw, square, triangle, noise, or freehand-drawn shapes), each at a chosen harmonic ratio, phase, and amplitude. The sum is baked into a single single-cycle waveform that the synth plays back.

Beyond single cycles, the editor supports:

  • Multi-frame wavetables: several distinct waveforms stacked into a grid, morphed between by Position knobs
  • N-dimensional grids: add Position knobs for 2D, 3D, or higher-dimensional morphing spaces
  • Scatter mode: instead of a grid, place frames freely in N-D space and morph between them via RBF blending — useful when your waveforms form a non-grid topology (e.g., three frames arranged in a triangle)
  • 3D anaglyph viewport for visualizing 3D-and-higher scatter spaces through red-cyan glasses
